05 / Due diligence, in plain language

Questions a serious buyer should ask.

Does Noetfield issue tokens, broker investments or hold client money?

No. This page offers technical assessment, engineering and integration services. Securities issuance, legal advice, custody and regulated payments require the appropriate parties and authorizations. No customer funds are requested through this page.

Is the RWA Clinic a regulatory approval?

No. It is a first-party technical assessment workflow with clearly defined gates and sample cases. A resulting verdict is not an approval by a regulator, a substitute for counsel or an offer to investors.

Can you work with our existing issuer, payment or smart-contract provider?

That is the preferred starting point where practical. We define interfaces and responsibilities first, then scope the integration. We do not claim any unverified partnership or provider approval.

What will you need for the first discussion?

A non-confidential description of the asset or workflow, the relevant jurisdiction, the systems involved, desired outcome and project owner. Do not send customer records, private keys, identity documents or confidential agreements in the initial form.

Are the demonstrations evidence of external customer deployments?

No. Demonstrations prove technical behavior within their described test scope. We make no claims of external client adoption, regulatory certification or collected revenue from those demos.

Do you offer a single global legal structure for tokenization?

No. Jurisdiction-specific rights, entity formation, securities analysis and financial-service obligations are determined by qualified professionals and relevant counterparties. Noetfield can translate approved requirements into technical systems.
