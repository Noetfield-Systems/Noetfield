Does tokenization automatically transfer legal ownership of a business or asset?

No. Legal rights depend on the underlying documents, registered interests, entity structure and applicable law. Technology implements documented rights; it does not replace legally effective records.

Does Noetfield hold crypto, provide custody, broker securities or close transactions?

This offer is for technical assessment, preparation and infrastructure engineering. Regulated securities activities, asset custody, conversion and closing, where applicable, require appropriately authorized counterparties. Any provider integration must be separately scoped.

Do I need a wallet or an existing token?

No. Begin with the economic problem. A wallet or token is considered only if necessary for the approved transaction or operating workflow.

Is an assessment equivalent to legal advice or regulatory approval?

No. The RWA Clinic and technical sprint are professional technology services. Legal, financial and regulated matters are handled by relevant qualified professionals or authorized providers.
