# Trust Ledger vs Trust Brief

## Trust Ledger — product infrastructure

The Trust Ledger is Noetfield's auditable governance decision record:

- Records decisions and policy references.
- Preserves Request ID (RID) lineage.
- Produces Trust Ledger Entries (TLEs).
- Supports board, procurement, and vendor-diligence exports.
- Public orientation: /trust-ledger/.

## Trust Brief — consulting engagement

Trust Brief is a six-week governance diagnostic. The current Stripe self-serve checkout is US$10,000. The engagement may include governance audit, policy mapping, risk exposure analysis, evidence expectations, and a decision memo as defined in the SOW.

Intake: /trust-brief/intake/

## Key difference

Trust Ledger is ongoing evidence infrastructure. Trust Brief is a scoped professional-services engagement. They are related but are not the same SKU.

## Commercial boundary

Noetfield sells software and professional services. It does not execute payments, custody assets, or provide legal/regulatory certification.
