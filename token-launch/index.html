05 / PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

What happens before launch?

Can Noetfield help even if I do not have a token or wallet?

Yes. Start with your use case and requirements. Tokenomics, chain selection and wallet architecture can be part of the technical scope. No wallet is needed for a project enquiry.

Can you work on community tokens, startup tokens and real-world assets?

We can assess and scope each category. Community and protocol tokens have different engineering needs from tokenized assets, where legal rights, issuer authority, recordkeeping and counterparties determine what technology may represent.

Do you provide token sales, guaranteed exchange listings or investor returns?

No. This clinic is a technical engineering and project-scoping service, not an exchange, investment solicitation, custody provider or promise of liquidity. Any issuance, sale or regulated activity needs properly authorized issuer and relevant providers.

How is the project priced?

Send a free initial request. We then define a written scope, deliverables, timing and price for your exact project. No payment is taken by this enquiry form. Work is charged only after a separate agreed engagement.

Can your team build the app and community alongside the token?

Software, web applications, campaign interfaces, wallet connection, claim preparation and managed technical operations can be scoped with the engineering work. Each capability is delivered with concrete acceptance criteria, not an unsupported launch promise.
