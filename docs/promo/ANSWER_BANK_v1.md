## 250-word description

Noetfield Systems Inc. is a Vancouver-based company building governed AI execution
infrastructure: systems where AI can act on consequential work while a separate
verifier, not the model, decides what passes.

The product surface has four parts. The Noetfield app (app.noetfield.com) is an
agentic workspace: give it a goal and it structures the work, runs a bounded team of
workers, checks the result against acceptance criteria, and keeps the evidence.
Runways are workflow classes that define how a result qualifies: inputs, acceptance
criteria, human decision points, and the receipt each run must leave behind. The
Deterministic API (developer preview) offers schema-valid-or-fail structured
completions under the same controls. AI Motors are the execution runtimes
underneath: explicit authority before action, policy and budget fencing, separate
verification, bounded repair, and a documented safe stop instead of a silent pass.

TrustField is Noetfield's regulated-operations and compliance product vertical. It
applies the governed execution infrastructure to consequential financial and
compliance workflows while preserving human decision authority; public
demonstrations use synthetic data and the human FILE / NO-FILE / ESCALATE decision
stays with the reporting entity.

Stage, stated plainly: the system is live as a founder-operated alpha. Evidence is
first-party and published in a public register (noetfield.com/proof/), including a
completed governed software change with verification, bounded repair, and the
receipt preserved. No external customers or revenue yet, and nothing beyond the
published evidence is claimed. A receipt is not certification.

## 500-word description

Use the 250-word description, then append:

Why this matters now: capable models are abundant; accountable execution is not.
Enterprises adopting agents face the same questions regulators and boards already
ask: who authorized this action, what evidence was inspected, what happened when it
failed, and who accepted the result. Most agent stacks answer none of these.
Noetfield makes the surrounding operating layer explicit without giving the model
authority over its own actions or approval.

How it works: execution starts from a scoped goal, not an open prompt. The system
resolves the deterministic envelope: applicable policy, delegated authority limits,
spend and time budget, and the context workers may see. Probabilistic workers do the
work inside that envelope. A separate verifier judges candidates against acceptance
criteria; repair is bounded, and when the bound is reached the system escalates or
stops safely. A human promotion decision closes the path, and the receipt preserves
what ran, which authority applied, what failed, how it was repaired, and why
promotion was accepted.

For platform companies (the primary partner thesis): Noetfield is deliberately
model-agnostic and positions as the independent verification layer, the party that
is not the model vendor and not the enterprise, whose judgment of pass/fail is
therefore worth keeping. Platforms supply capability; Noetfield supplies the
governance harness, agentic workflow structure, and receipt layer their enterprise
and institutional customers need before consequential deployment.

Company: founded and operated by Sina Kazemnezhad (LLB) in Vancouver, British
Columbia. The founder's legal background shapes the design principle that governance
belongs inside execution, not only in policy documents or after-the-fact reviews.
