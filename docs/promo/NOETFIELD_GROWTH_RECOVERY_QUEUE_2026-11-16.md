## Founder doctrine — preserve both pillars, never trade one against the other

### 1. Visual authority: image first

Noetfield must look like a credible, ambitious real-economy technology company. PR #388 V5 shows a *large real-world urban photographic panorama*, real software screenshot, documentary industrial and architectural assets, cinematic middle chapter and coherent corporate conclusion. This is the accepted **human-facing visual grammar**.

Use **large editorial imagery, scarce but strong words, cinematic hierarchy, whitespace and polished typography**. DO NOT replace the Hero with a generic heading stack, cards, overlaid copy, 3D orbit/rings, pyramids, floating panels, decorative cubes, abstract AI blobs or a technical dashboard. Do not invent office/property ownership from editorial photographs. No new rendered images are requested here.

### 2. Economic authority: make useful things happen

Positioning axis: **Intelligence for the Real Economy**. Deeper aim: **Build, run and grow intelligent businesses; connect the real and digital economies.**

The corporation makes software and businesses more useful, connects human operators with intelligent execution, and prepares real economic value for digital infrastructure. The product story must encompass **people, law and commercial realities, company operations, financial/economic opportunity, technology and new ventures**—not only onchain, governance or agent-internal instrumentation.

- **Noetfield App:** actual AI software creation experience; flagship customer-accessible product.
- **Company OS:** automate and operate real businesses; practical scoped business workflows and services.
- **Real-World Capital:** technology readiness, rights/workflow mapping, private-credit and acquisition preparation. This is an important potential paid lane, not the entire parent identity.
- **Noetfield Runtime / Verifier / AgentOS:** execution and evidence infrastructure, subordinate to the human benefit in buyer-facing language.
- **People & Ventures:** entrepreneurs, specialists, partners and communities bring capability and economic opportunities. Independent ventures are not automatically parent-company products, securities or owned communities.

## Implementation isolation rules — protect the work, not bureaucracy

1. **Do not modify:** `index.html`, `assets/noetfield-institutional-v3.css`, the V5 visual layout, photo composition or original screenshot assets. An explicit, specific Founder instruction is needed to change the approved visual.
2. **Do not copy:** the `nr-hero-stage`, circles/orbits, CSS theme, large floating text cards and replacement navigation from PR #390. The rejected presentation is NOT a template for inner pages.
3. **Do retain:** the usefulness of the strategy and functional ideas. Implement them with short copy and real user actions on their relevant existing inner routes, one independent scope at a time.
4. **Do not merge feature restorations as one sitewide refactor.** All touched files must be listed and tested; if a page fails, revert only that page's delta, not the work on unrelated product journeys.
5. **Protect real conversion:** Existing forms, consent, privacy, prices, payment links, and available product paths must function after each change. No automatic wallet gate.
6. **No fake success metrics:** Distinguish page published, feature implemented, form saved, reply received, meeting booked, scope signed, payment settled, and verified delivery.
7. **No new design work or site modifications are authorized by this recovery queue.** It records existing Founder decisions and candidates for future scoped execution.
