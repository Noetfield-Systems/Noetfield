## REASON
The old kit had strong hooks but repeated the same four-input, two-field-notes, free/no-signup formula seven times. It assumed that content automatically creates inbound demand, included unsupported generalizations, and misclassified labor usage as ungoverned. The goal is neither reach nor a platform tour: it is a paid, bounded workflow engagement from an evidence-driven buyer.

## GOAL
**Message:** Know the cost. Name the owner. Prove the outcome.
**Category:** Workflow execution accountability (not "AI tools" or automatic certification).
**Promise:** A decision that can defensibly be *leave alone*, *fix existing process*, or *scope a governed implementation*. Illustrative calculators are not audits, quotes, or savings forecasts.
**Positioning:** Don't automate a problem until you can price it. Don't trust an AI-assisted deliverable until someone is authorized to accept it.

## OUTCOME: three commercial motions
1. **Ops:** Identify a repetitive workflow with measurable manual effort. Ask for one bounded process; do not prescribe technology up front.
2. **Finance:** Attribute material AI spend to workflows and owners. Show unattributed *exposure*, not waste.
3. **Risk / engineering / board:** Identify acceptance evidence, authority boundaries, failure records, and whether the verifier is separate from the executor.
Partner motion: fractional CFOs, COO advisers, MSPs and specialist consultants can share/embed the checks, with an honest attribution line.

**Only one public commercial ask:** "Bring one workflow, a current process owner and a redacted example. Request a scoped review." Route via the existing https://www.noetfield.com/contact/ (or an actually commissioned offer page, once built). Do NOT describe the diagnostic as scheduled, free, independently audited, or immediately bookable unless verified. Offer a signed scope and fixed deliverables only after technical qualification.

## EDITORIAL RULES
- Start with a consequential observation, not "I built a tool" in every post.
- Never claim clients, case studies, benchmark percentages or behavioral prevalence without evidence.
- "$X a year" in these calculators is a *scenario* derived from user inputs, not automatically recoverable cash.
- 1.3 loaded rate, 48 working weeks, $3K/yr and $1.5K/mo cutoffs are illustrative heuristics. They do not overrule high-consequence compliance/security issues.
- Show real UI captures from current live tools with demonstrative input and a "SAMPLE SCENARIO" label. Never show a fictional customer account or invented production receipt.
- One primary point and one CTA per post. No seven consecutive mini-sales pitches.
- Preserve privacy: no users' cost figures, named people, or workflow secrets in social screenshots. Share URLs contain input values; treat them as public.
- Claim only demonstrated product status: internal first-party verification is not external adoption or certification.
- Do not buy follower engagement, fabricate replies, or use fake buyers.

## COMMERCIAL CONVERSION DESIGN
**Traffic:** public posts, real comments, personal follow-up, partner embeds. No fake social proof.
**Tool completion:** compute locally; no email wall.
**Self-selection:** Leave alone → takeaway. Improve current process → action checklist. Consequential or material gap → optional scope discussion.
**Qualification form (proposed):** work email (opt-in), role, industry, workflow name (non-confidential), volume, systems touched, decision owner, risk class, preferred outcome, timeframe, potential budget, and permission to contact. No customer data uploads required for the first conversation.
**Discovery:** confirm measured baseline, authority, data classification, acceptance conditions, and buying group; reject poor-fit leads.
**Paid scope:** bounded deliverables, access boundary, success criteria, cost limit, independent check, evidence receipt and sign-off. Propose an SOW; do not promise a product's maturity beyond published scope.
**Follow-up:** one human reply within an internally committed SLA, a one-page fit memo, then a concrete accept/decline decision.
**Revenue recognition:** only signed contracts and actual payments count, not calculators, leads, or hypothetical tenants.

Instrument *only what the site privacy notice permits*. Until privacy and implementation are explicitly reviewed, use link UTM attribution + existing aggregate infrastructure reporting + prospect opt-in; do not send form inputs or numerical results to third-party analytics.

UTM convention (for each link): `utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=operator_evidence_2026q4&utm_content=p1_quiet_leak`; change p1→p7 identifier per post. Avoid attaching sensitive query-parameter form values to social links.

Single operating record per lead:
`source_post, campaign, persona, organization, permission_to_contact, workflow, status, qualifying_evidence, owner, next_action_date, opportunity_value, contract_state, cash_collected`.
No automated lead scores disguised as fact.

**Weekly funnel:** meaningful replies → consented conversations → qualified workflows → scoping calls → sent scopes → signed SOW → cash received.
**Initial experiments, not forecasts:** 7 posts; 30–50 contextual operator interactions; 8–12 relevant advisor introductions; goal 3 qualified workflow conversations; goal one scoped proposal. Reassess after two weeks. Never call this traction until observed.
**Kill rule:** if qualified conversations are zero after 4 posts plus 20 genuinely contextual outreaches, change the hook/ICP and CTA before posting another generic tool roundup.
**Opportunity rule:** if finance replies but risk owners do not, lead with financial attribution and offer one-workflow assurance as the second step; reverse if GRC demand appears first.
