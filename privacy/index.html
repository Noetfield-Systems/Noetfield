Privacy

How we handle information.

This notice describes what the Noetfield corporate site and product surfaces collect, why, and how to reach us — without marketing offers or product SKUs.

Updated: 13 November 2026

Who we are

Noetfield Systems Inc., Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada. Corporate site: www.noetfield.com (marketing, proof, intake). Product: app.noetfield.com (authenticated sessions, projects, execution).

Privacy contact: operations@noetfield.com · Privacy intake form

What we collect

Public contact and project intake — name, work email, organization when applicable, inquiry topic, proposal and messages submitted through Contact, Work With Us and Connect. Records are stored by the existing Noetfield platform intake service.

Connect contributor and opportunity context — depending on your selected route, we may collect skills, availability, preferred work model, project interests, location, languages, portfolio and LinkedIn URLs, referral source, conversation context, business constraints or capital-interest context. Do not submit passwords, private conversations, financial account details or confidential third-party records.

Case status and feedback — a request reference and the email associated with the submitted intake allow you to request a limited status summary, and to send feedback on the same intake record. Feedback includes any rating or message you submit. This is not an identity-verified account portal and does not expose internal reviewer notes or decisions.

Request IDs (RID) — generated in your browser by noetfield-shell.js and stored in localStorage under key nf_rid. RIDs thread async intake and are echoed in form payloads. We do not use RID as a first-party HTTP cookie on canonical corporate pages.

Cookie consent preference (www) — if shown, stored in localStorage as noetfield_cookie_consent_v1 by noetfield-cookie-consent.js.

Product accounts (app) — when you use app.noetfield.com, we process account identifiers, session tokens, project metadata, and execution records required to operate governed workflows.

Analytics and tracking

No third-party advertising trackers are required for the corporate intake journeys. Connect sends first-party journey events only when the visitor explicitly opts in. Event data may include the selected route, campaign and source identifiers, page path and an ephemeral session reference; event tracking is not a hiring or payment event. Infrastructure providers (including Cloudflare and Railway) may process standard HTTP metadata such as IP addresses, user agents and timestamps for security and operations.

How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, assess and route opportunities, contact applicants, support authorized collaboration, operate product sessions, secure services and improve reliability. Where a contributor explicitly requests and consents to internal matching, qualifying Connect data may be forwarded to the connected WorkforceOS operation under Noetfield control; this does not itself confer an assignment, job, or contract. We do not sell personal information.

Storage and subprocessors

Intake and product data are processed on infrastructure operated by Noetfield and contracted providers used in current deployment manifests, including Cloudflare (static site and edge) and Railway (platform API and runtime). Data may be processed in Canada and the United States depending on provider region configuration.

Retention

Intake, qualification, status and feedback records are kept for operational response, relevant follow-up and an accountable review trail; product execution records support continuity and evidence replay. We do not state a fixed deletion interval that the current system does not enforce. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion; applicable operational and legal retention requirements may limit deletion.

Your requests

Access, correction, or deletion requests: Contact operations with your Request ID when available. We respond within a reasonable period.

Changes

We update this notice when collection, infrastructure, or retention practices change. Canonical URL: https://www.noetfield.com/privacy/
