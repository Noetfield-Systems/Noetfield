Questions worth asking

Software cannot replace authority.

The process may only need an owner, better documentation or a clear acceptance test. We should be willing to recommend that.

Does Company OS send messages without approval?

No. A defined role does not grant outbound permission. External messages require the appropriate authorization, identity and operational configuration.

Do I need to replace my CRM or helpdesk?

No. Existing tools are the starting point; any connector has to pass privacy, access and workflow checks.

Is this a finished self-running enterprise product?

No. Existing Noetfield components and agent runtimes are at different levels of maturity. Scope is commissioned and verified step by step, and the alpha is founder-operated.
