Assurance · The acceptance layer

The agent should not be the final judge of its own work.

Capable agents are abundant. What remains scarce is a credible answer to the questions every consequential deployment gets asked: who authorized the action, what exactly was produced, who accepted it, and what evidence remains. Noetfield builds that answer into execution itself.

The assurance layer

The acceptance, evidence, and improvement plane for agentic execution: explicit authority before action, separate acceptance of the exact result, bounded repair, and replayable evidence before anything is promoted. Provider- and model-neutral by design.

The runtime

Governed agent runtimes and the execution control plane underneath: AI Motors. Technical architecture

What acceptance means here

The sequence: agent runtime → exact artifact/effect binding → separate verifier → ACCEPT / REJECT / REPAIR / ESCALATE / STOP / NOT_EVALUABLE → external promotion authority → replayable receipt.

Every governed run follows the same path: an authorized event; an authority, policy, and budget envelope; bounded execution; exact binding of the candidate result; evaluation by a verifier that is separate from the worker that produced it; bounded repair, escalation, or a documented safe stop; a human promotion decision outside both executor and verifier; and a machine-readable receipt. Missing required evidence fails closed. A receipt is not certification.

Watch a complete run: a full first-party pass of this path on a real change, published with its verification history. The company-stage page states exactly what first-party operation proves and does not prove.

What exists, exactly

Proven first-party

RUNNING Separate-verifier acceptance, bounded repair with documented safe stops, exact-artifact binding by digest, human promotion outside executor and verifier, and inspectable failed attempts. These run on Noetfield's own delivery today, receipts published.

Internally demonstrated

FIRST-PARTY The complete governed replacement run, escalation practice, and the release pipeline's claim gates. First-party evidence, stated limits: internal use does not prove external adoption.

Portability still in development

IN DESIGN A reusable third-party Acceptance Gateway API/SDK, independent conformance tests and externally attested verifier identity are not established by the currently deployed internal API.

Missing for portability

NOT YET External third-party proof, security review, cross-platform conformance testing, and attestable verifier identity. Stated plainly so nothing here overstates.
